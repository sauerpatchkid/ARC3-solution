# S5 v2: colour no-ops judged at the level's end — check (3 Nov 2026)

*Exploratory, not pre-registered; it changes no verdict. The S5 change that
`dc22-noop-check.md` ("What it means") proposed, designed with Matt on 3 Nov
as dev sweep 2's candidate. Tuned on dev-game recordings (G2's and A0's), and
labelled so wherever it is used.*

## In one line

**With S5 v2, online prune should match what the counterfactual said.**
Replaying G2's GB-free runs with the agent's own mask, v1 reproduces what those
runs carried online (9 of 10 recordings exactly). v2 carries the
counterfactual's colours on all 10: m0r0 7 and 3 instead of 1 and 0, cd82 3
instead of 0, vc33 a level earlier. Avoidable waste rises to the
counterfactual's: vc33 29.0% → 38.2%, m0r0 0.1% → 6.5%, dc22 1.6% → 5.3%,
cd82 0 → 3.1%. Masking stays ≤ 0.05%. On A0's 24 dev recordings, v2 under the
agent's mask gives prereg 5a's numbers exactly. Whether more prune means more
levels is dev sweep 2's question (prereg 7a).

## The problem

S5 v1 carries a colour as a no-op when, on one level, it was clicked at least
20 times and no click on it changed a live cell. Online, each click was judged
when it was made, under the agent's own decoration mask at that moment. That
mask is empty for the run's first 200 moves (`EVAL_CANON_WARMUP`) and lacks
the last cells of bars that empty only late in an attempt (m0r0's move-budget
bar, which G0 found). So one early click that moved only a decoration kept a
colour from being carried for the whole level. The offline counterfactual
(prereg 5a) judged clicks under S0's mask and so overstated online prune:
colours carried online vs in the replay, on G2's GB-free runs, were m0r0 1 vs 7
and 0 vs 3, cd82 0 vs 3, vc33 the same 7 but a level late (point 3 of
`dc22-noop-check.md`).

## What changed (`custom_agents/gb/s5_act.py`, VERSION 2)

Two settings in `[act]`, both off in the code (so v1 is still what an old
config file gets) and on in `glassbox.toml` from this commit:

- **`colour_judge = "level_end"`.** For each colour, S5 keeps the cells its
  clicks changed (raw, no mask). At the level's end the colour is carried if it
  was clicked at least 20 times and none of those cells is live under the mask
  of that moment, with S0's bars run to the screen's edges
  (`s0_mask.complete_bars`). Online that mask is the agent's at the level-up;
  in the replay it is S0's mask of the level, as before. v1 (`"click"`) judged
  each click when it was made.
- **`mask_bars = true`.** The mask the guide judges every move under online is
  the agent's (its canonicaliser's mask and the bars upgrade's) with S0's bars
  run to the edges. This one was added after the first check below: once v2
  carried the replay's colours, the online runs **dropped** most of them again
  within a few hundred moves, and every drop came from a click that changed
  only the last cell of a bar on row 63: m0r0 (63, 58) at moves 147 and 138 of
  level 2 (the end of the move-budget bar G0 found), dc22 (63, 63) and (63, 62)
  at moves 190 and 376. Each of those cells is in S0's mask and in the agent's
  mask once its bars are completed. Dropping itself is unchanged: a click on a
  carried colour that changes a live cell drops it at once.

Nothing else moves: the book's no-op rules (re-bound on S0's mask, as
before), goal hints, the map, alpha, the 20-click minimum. The agent is not
touched: `s5_act` is imported only when `EVAL_GB=1` (`custom_agents/action.py`,
the `# [gb]` lines are unchanged), so no `cpu_check` is needed.

## How it was checked

All on the CPU, no GPU, no LLM (`results/glassbox/s5v2_check/run.sh` and
`run_b.sh`, 3 Nov, about 25 min in all).

1. **A replay that sees what the agent saw.** The replay gained `[act]
   replay_mask = "agent"`: the guide is told whether a move changed anything
   under the agent's own mask, rebuilt move by move from the recording with the
   agent's own classes (`OnlineCanonicalizer`, `BarDetector`, fed in the order
   `action.py` feeds them), while avoidable waste and masking are still judged
   under S0's mask. Replaying an online S5 run, the replay also lists what that
   run carried online (its `glassbox/events.jsonl`). First test: v1 with the
   agent's mask must reproduce what G2's GB-free runs carried online.
2. **Does v2 close the gap?** The same 10 recordings (G2's GB-free runs of the
   5 click games, seeds 0-1, no book) replayed with v2, with the agent's mask,
   against v1 under S0's mask (the 5a counterfactual's view).
3. **Masking stays near 0.** GB with the pooled book on A0's 24 dev recordings
   (5a's setup, `--cf`): v2 under S0's mask, and v2 with the agent's mask.

With `mask_bars`, every number in 5a's summary is reproduced, and so is every

## What it means

- **S5 v2 = `colour_judge = "level_end"` + `mask_bars = true` is dev sweep 2's
  candidate** (`glassbox.toml` config `8c8e1cb023c827d2`, `[act]`
  `bcf6c0c0e4c8bd94`; prereg 7a). It is tuned on dev-game recordings and
  labelled so.
- **The counterfactual's numbers are now the online expectation:** in each new
  level's first 2,000 actions, the guide should cover about 60% of vc33's moves,
  23% of ft09's, 8% of dc22's, 6% of cd82's and 5% of m0r0's, all of them moves
  that do nothing. Under v1, online, vc33 got about a level less of it, and
  m0r0, dc22 and cd82 almost none.
- **What it does not change:** levels are a separate question. G2's GB (34)
  and GB-free (35) were within noise of A0 run again (33), and more pruned dead
  clicks may still not mean more levels. Dev sweep 2 measures that against a
  same-day A0.
- **A tool for later:** `--set act.replay_mask="agent"` replays any recording as
  the agent would have judged it, so an online S5 change can be checked on the
  CPU before it costs GPU time.
- **Not changed:** the agent's own mask (`canon.py`, the novelty key and the
  map) still lacks those bar ends. v2 changes only what S5 sees. The baseline is
  not touched.
