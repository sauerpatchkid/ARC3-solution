    What each column counts
    S1 explains it
    S1 (no LLM) describes the change as a short list (at most 8) of whole objects that moved, recoloured, grew, shrank, vanished or appeared, and that list rebuilds the screen. Where this is low, perception is the bottleneck, not the LLM.
    In a group the LLM was asked about
    Changing cases in an action group (a button, or clicks on one colour) that got LLM calls.
    An admitted rule explains part of it
    Some admitted rule applies, gets at least one changed cell right, and gets every cell it claims right. Admitted rules can still be wrong elsewhere, so they are not in the book.
    The book explains part, nothing wrong
    The book (the trusted rules, composed cell by cell) gets at least one changed cell right and no claimed cell wrong.
    The book gets the whole screen right
    Every live cell of the predicted screen matches what happened (v1's strict number, "case coverage").
    Cell coverage
    Of all changed cells on the level, the share the book claims and gets right. Glassbox's G1 primary.
    T1 wrong
    Share of the next level's changing cases with a wrong claimed cell. The safety bar is 5%.
    Per move right; T1 − baseline
    Share of recorded moves whose whole next screen T1 predicts, beside "nothing changes" and "memory" (repeat what this screen and action did before). The 95% interval resamples attempts.
